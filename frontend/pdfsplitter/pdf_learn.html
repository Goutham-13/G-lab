📄 What is a PDF Splitter?

A PDF Splitter allows you to divide a single PDF into multiple smaller PDFs by specifying page ranges. This helps organize, share, or extract content easily.

⚙️ How It Works

  1. Upload your PDF file.
  2. Specify the number of splits you want.
  3. Enter the start and end page for each split.
  4. Click Generate Splits and download the resulting PDFs.

💡 Tips for Best Results

  • Ensure page ranges are correct and do not exceed total pages.
  • Overlapping pages will show a confirmation before splitting.
  • Use this tool for large PDFs to extract only the needed sections.

🔒 Privacy First

Astra’s PDF Splitter works locally in your browser — your files are never uploaded to a server. Everything is processed on your device securely.
