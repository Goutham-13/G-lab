Understand how Astra’s QR Generator works and how to use it effectively.

🔍 What is a QR Code?

A QR (Quick Response) code is a digital pattern that stores data like text, URLs, or messages. Scanning it with a smartphone instantly reveals the embedded content.

⚙️ How It Works

  1. Type a text, URL, or message into the input box.
  2. Astra instantly generates a QR image using fast, local JavaScript rendering.
  3. You can download the generated QR as a PNG file.

💡 Tips for Best Results

  • Keep your text short for clear, scannable results.
  • Use dark colors on a light background for better visibility.
  • Always test your QR before sharing.

🔒 Privacy First

Astra’s QR Generator works locally — your data never leaves your browser. Nothing is stored or sent to any server.
